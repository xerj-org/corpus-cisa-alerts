# CISA Releases Five Industrial Control Systems Advisories

Release Date

November 04, 2025

CISA released five Industrial Control Systems (ICS) Advisories. These advisories provide timely information about current security issues, vulnerabilities, and exploits surrounding ICS.

*   ICSA-25-308-01 [Fuji Electric Monitouch V-SFT-6](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories/icsa-25-308-01 "Fuji Electric Monitouch V-SFT-6")
*   ICSA-25-308-02 [Survision License Plate Recognition Camera](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories/icsa-25-308-02 "Survision License Plate Recognition Camera")
*   ICSA-25-308-03 [Delta Electronics CNCSoft-G2](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories/icsa-25-308-03 "Delta Electronics CNCSoft-G2")
*   ICSA-25-308-04 [Radiometrics VizAir](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories/icsa-25-308-04 "Radiometrics VizAir")
*   ICSA-25-308-05 [IDIS ICM Viewer](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories/icsa-25-308-05 "IDIS ICM Viewer")

CISA encourages users and administrators to review newly released ICS Advisories for technical details and mitigations.
