5.    CISA Releases Six Industrial Control Systems Advisories 

Alert

Release Date

November 18, 2025

Related topics:

[Industrial Control System Vulnerabilities](https://www.cisa.gov/topics/industrial-control-systems/industrial-control-system-vulnerabilities) , [Industrial Control Systems](https://www.cisa.gov/topics/industrial-control-systems)

CISA released six Industrial Control Systems (ICS) Advisories. These advisories provide timely information about current security issues, vulnerabilities, and exploits surrounding ICS.

*   ICSA-25-322-01 [Schneider Electric EcoStruxure Machine SCADA Expert & Pro-face BLUE Open Studio](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories/icsa-25-322-01 "Schneider Electric EcoStruxure Machine SCADA Expert & Pro-face BLUE Open Studio")
*   ICSA-25-322-02 [Shelly Pro 4PM](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories/icsa-25-322-02 "Shelly Pro 4PM")
*   ICSA-25-322-03 [Shelly Pro 3EM](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories/icsa-25-322-03 "Shelly Pro 3EM")
*   ICSA-25-322-04 [Schneider Electric PowerChute Serial Shutdown](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories/icsa-25-322-04 "Schneider Electric PowerChute Serial Shutdown")
*   ICSA-25-322-05 [METZ CONNECT EWIO2](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories/icsa-25-322-05 "METZ CONNECT EWIO2")
*   ICSA-25-224-03 [Schneider Electric EcoStruxure (Update B)](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories/icsa-25-224-03 "Schneider Electric EcoStruxure (Update B)")

CISA encourages users and administrators to review newly released ICS Advisories for technical details and mitigations.
