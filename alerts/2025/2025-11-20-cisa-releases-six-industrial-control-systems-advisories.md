5.    CISA Releases Six Industrial Control Systems Advisories 

Alert

Release Date

November 20, 2025

Related topics:

[Industrial Control System Vulnerabilities](https://www.cisa.gov/topics/industrial-control-systems/industrial-control-system-vulnerabilities) , [Industrial Control Systems](https://www.cisa.gov/topics/industrial-control-systems)

CISA released six Industrial Control Systems (ICS) Advisories. These advisories provide timely information about current security issues, vulnerabilities, and exploits surrounding ICS.

*   ICSA-25-324-01 [Automated Logic WebCTRL Premium Server](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories/icsa-25-324-01 "Automated Logic WebCTRL Premium Server")
*   ICSA-25-324-02 [ICAM365 CCTV Camera Multiple Models](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories/icsa-25-324-02 "ICAM365 CCTV Camera Multiple Models")
*   ICSA-25-324-03 [Opto 22 GRV-EPIC and GRV-RIO](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories/icsa-25-324-03 "Opto 22 GRV-EPIC and GRV-RIO")
*   ICSA-25-324-04 [Festo MSE6-C2M/D2M/E2M](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories/icsa-25-324-04 "Festo MSE6-C2M/D2M/E2M")
*   ICSA-25-324-05 [Festo Didactic products](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories/icsa-25-324-05 "Festo Didactic products")
*   ICSA-25-324-06 [Emerson Appleton UPSMON-PRO](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories/icsa-25-324-06 "Emerson Appleton UPSMON-PRO")

CISA encourages users and administrators to review newly released ICS Advisories for technical details and mitigations.
