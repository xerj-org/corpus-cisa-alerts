5.    CISA Releases Nine Industrial Control Systems Advisories 

Alert

Release Date

December 04, 2025

Related topics:

[Industrial Control System Vulnerabilities](https://www.cisa.gov/topics/industrial-control-systems/industrial-control-system-vulnerabilities) , [Industrial Control Systems](https://www.cisa.gov/topics/industrial-control-systems)

CISA released nine Industrial Control Systems (ICS) Advisories. These advisories provide timely information about current security issues, vulnerabilities, and exploits surrounding ICS.

*   ICSA-25-338-01 [Mitsubishi Electric GX Works2](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories/icsa-25-338-01 "Mitsubishi Electric GX Works2")
*   ICSA-25-338-02 [MAXHUB Pivot](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories/icsa-25-338-02 "MAXHUB Pivot")
*   ICSA-25-338-03 [Johnson Controls OpenBlue Mobile Web Application for OpenBlue Workplace](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories/icsa-25-338-03 "Johnson Controls OpenBlue Mobile Web Application for OpenBlue Workplace")
*   ICSA-25-338-04 [Johnson Controls iSTAR](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories/icsa-25-338-04 "Johnson Controls iSTAR")
*   ICSA-25-338-05 [Sunbird DCIM dcTrack and Power IQ](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories/icsa-25-338-05 "Sunbird DCIM dcTrack and Power IQ")
*   ICSA-25-338-06 [SolisCloud Monitoring Platform](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories/icsa-25-338-06 "SolisCloud Monitoring Platform")
*   ICSA-25-338-07 [Advantech iView](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories/icsa-25-338-07 "Advantech iView")
*   ICSA-25-148-03 [Consilium Safety CS5000 Fire Panel (Update A)](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories/icsa-25-338-07 "Consilium Safety CS5000 Fire Panel (Update A)")
*   ICSA-25-219-02 [Johnson Controls FX Server, FX80 and FX90 (Update A)](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories/icsa-25-219-02 "Johnson Controls FX Server, FX80 and FX90 (Update A)")

CISA encourages users and administrators to review newly released ICS Advisories for technical details and mitigations.
