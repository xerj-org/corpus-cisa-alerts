# CISA Releases Three Industrial Control Systems Advisories

Release Date

December 09, 2025

 Related topics: 

[Industrial Control System Vulnerabilities](https://www.cisa.gov/topics/industrial-control-systems/industrial-control-system-vulnerabilities) , [Industrial Control Systems](https://www.cisa.gov/topics/industrial-control-systems)

CISA released three Industrial Control Systems (ICS) Advisories. These advisories provide timely information about current security issues, vulnerabilities, and exploits surrounding ICS.

*   ICSA-25-343-01 [Universal Boot Loader (U-Boot)](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories/icsa-25-343-01 "Universal Boot Loader (U-Boot)")
*   ICSA-25-343-02 [Festo LX Appliance](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories/icsa-25-343-02 "Festo LX Appliance")
*   ICSA-25-343-03 [Multiple India-Based CCTV Cameras](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories/icsa-25-343-03 "Multiple India-Based CCTV Cameras")

CISA encourages users and administrators to review newly released ICS Advisories for technical details and mitigations.
