# CISA Releases Nine Industrial Control Systems Advisories

Release Date

December 18, 2025

 Related topics: 

[Industrial Control System Vulnerabilities](https://www.cisa.gov/topics/industrial-control-systems/industrial-control-system-vulnerabilities) , [Industrial Control Systems](https://www.cisa.gov/topics/industrial-control-systems)

CISA released nine Industrial Control Systems (ICS) Advisories. These advisories provide timely information about current security issues, vulnerabilities, and exploits surrounding ICS.

*   ICSA-25-352-01 [Inductive Automation Ignition](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories/icsa-25-352-01 "Inductive Automation Ignition")
*   ICSA-25-352-02 [Schneider Electric EcoStruxure Foxboro DCS Advisor](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories/icsa-25-352-02 "Schneider Electric EcoStruxure Foxboro DCS Advisor")
*   ICSA-25-352-03 [National Instruments LabView](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories/icsa-25-352-03 "National Instruments LabView")
*   ICSA-25-352-04 [Mitsubishi Electric Iconics Digital Solutions and Mitsubishi Electrics Products](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories/icsa-25-352-04 "Mitsubishi Electric Iconics Digital Solutions and Mitsubishi Electrics Products")
*   ICSA-25-352-05 [Siemens Interniche IP-Stack](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories/icsa-25-352-05 "Siemens Interniche IP-Stack")
*   ICSA-25-352-06 [Advantech WebAccess/SCADA](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories/icsa-25-352-06 "Advantech WebAccess/SCADA")
*   ICSA-25-352-07 [Rockwell Automation Micro820, Micro850, Micro 870](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories/icsa-25-352-07 "Rockwell Automation Micro820, Micro850, Micro 870")
*   ICSA-25-352-08 [Axis Communications Camera Station Pro, Camera Station, and Device Manager](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories/icsa-25-352-08 "Axis Communications Camera Station Pro, Camera Station, and Device Manager")
*   ICSA-24-291-03 [Mitsubishi Electric CNC Series (Update C)](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories/icsa-24-291-03 "Mitsubishi Electric CNC Series (Update C)")

CISA encourages users and administrators to review newly released ICS Advisories for technical details and mitigations.
